# Pixel Pet Clinic changelog

## v0.2.1

- **Removing objects and surgery: your finger no longer hides the object.** Drag anywhere on the picture and the object moves the same way, like a trackpad, so you can keep your finger below the patient and watch the path. A ring follows the object and an arrow marks the way out.

## v0.2.0

Harder medicine, more to do after hours, and a lot more polish.

- **Diagnosing is a real puzzle now.** Five conditions per species (25 in all), and their signs overlap: a lump might be a swallowed sock, a gel growth or a bud about to split off. Each patient only shows some of its signs, sometimes alongside a harmless quirk (healthy Static Cats crackle a little; Fernlings droop in the afternoon).
- **Tests give readings, not answers.** Temperatures, weights, heart rates, sample panels and voltages are numbers you check against the handbook's normal ranges. You read X-rays yourself and tap what's wrong. The stethoscope sounds different in different places.
- **Every species has its own tests.** Gloops: jiggle test, backlight, pH strip. Pebblets: knock test, candling, the scale. Static Cats: stroke test and a voltmeter (watch out for hot spots). Fernlings: flip the leaves, soil probe, unpot the roots. Mothhounds: dim the lights, count breaths, wing-dust slide.
- **Severity.** Mild and severe cases need different treatments (tongs or surgery, a pill or a soak, fewer or more nights in the kennels). A test tells you which.
- **Stress.** Every test upsets the patient. Push too far and the Gloop splits, the cat shocks you, the Mothhound bolts for the lamp. Soothe them, or brew a tonic.
- **Owners fib**, or leave out the important part.
- **The handbook has torn pages.** Find them around town at night.
- **Dosing:** weigh the patient, then pick the dose. Too little doesn't work; too much makes them sick.
- **Triage:** patients get worse if they wait too long, and emergencies won't wait at all.
- **Kennels:** contagious guests spread to the cage next door. An isolation crate and mite smoke help.
- **Staff:** hire Dee (nurse) or Marco (receptionist), from Tuesday.
- **Equipment:** digital X-ray, electronic stethoscope, a better incubator, an isolation crate, a soothing lamp.
- **Patient charts** for returning patients. **Weather** that changes what comes in, and a **static flu outbreak** midweek.
- **After hours:** two strange patients knock after dark. They aren't in the handbook.
- **Evenings:** walk the town at night (Elm Park, the diner, Lou's Cards, Pixel Palace arcade, Lucky Strike Lanes, PetCorp, the back alley) to forage, find handbook pages, hear rumors, gather evidence on PetCorp, and find strays.
- **Brewing:** six remedies from foraged ingredients and patient leftovers, with a heat-control mini-game. Remedies make treatments easier, replace pills, or stop kennel spread.
- **Fostering:** look after strays and find them the right home at Saturday's adoption booth.
- **Saturday:** the town fair. Judge the pet show (someone's hiding something), run a market stall, or make house calls to Lou's Cards and Lucky Strike Lanes.
- **New title screen**, music for every part of the day, ambience (fluorescent hum, rain, the waiting room, crickets), new sound effects, pixel icons, patient portraits, and a settings screen with volume controls.
- Rent is now $400, and treatments pay a little less: the money should feel tighter.
- Saves from v0.1.0 can't carry over.

## v0.1.0

The week one demo.

- Monday to Friday at the clinic, with rent of $300 due Friday night.
- Five species and fifteen conditions, each with its own signs, sounds, temperature, X-ray, sample and mood.
- Lamp-lit exam room: signs of illness only show where the lamp is close. Eggs can be turned to see every side.
- Six treatment mini-games, plus the incubator and breaking bad news gently.
- Waiting room with patients who arrive through the day and lose patience.
- Clinic diorama for mornings and evenings, with kennel care, mail and supplies.
- Casebook with 3D turnarounds of every species and a wall of photos.
- Story: Dr. Okafor's letters, Tobi's egg, Mrs. Fenwick, Mr. Vance, PetCorp Wellness and the basement door.
