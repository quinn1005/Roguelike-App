Pet Clinic Art Tests

Five ways to make pixel art look 3D. Each screen is drawn live by code at the game's real low resolution, then scaled up with hard pixel edges, so every square you see is one real game pixel. Pick a theme to relight all five at once.

These are code-drawn stand-ins for comparing techniques. Hand-drawn sprites in the real game would carry far more detail.

℞Prescription

No single style does everything well. Here's how I'd mix them, one style per kind of screen:

Exam table
A, lamp-lit sprites. It's where you spend most of the game, and moving the lamp to examine a patient becomes part of play.
Clinic overview
C, tilt-shift diorama. The "wow" shot between patients, with the day passing through the window.
Close inspection
B, stacked slices for eggs, items and carriers you turn in your hands, and D, pre-rendered turnarounds for casebook entries.
Waiting room and town
E, parallax. Cheap to make and good for walking between places.
Themes
Use them as times of day: Sunday Morning when you open, Fluorescent through clinic hours, Grimy Night for the late shift, and After Hours for cursed-night story events.
