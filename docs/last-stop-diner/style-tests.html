Five looks for the parking lot brawl

Same scene in every style: the Line Brawler and Hedge Witch take on the Gristle Golem outside the diner. Everything you see is drawn and animated by code in real time, with no hand-made art. Tell me which ones you like. Mixing is fine, for example D's lighting with E's outlines.

Slow motion is the best way to see hit-stop, squash and smear frames.

Where code-drawn art tops out

  • D is about my ceiling. The lighting, bloom, particles, hit-stop and shading are close to shipping quality. The character shapes are built from ellipses and capsules, so they look clean but a bit "assembled".
  • A pixel artist adds the soul. Hand-placed pixels, expressive faces, anticipation and follow-through drawn frame by frame, and real costume detail. That is the gap between D and the games you mentioned.
  • The pipeline carries over. If you hire an artist later, their sprite sheets drop into the same engine and keep all of D's lighting and effects.
