The card lines

In Mint Condition you work the desk at Gemline Authentication, grading cards, catching fakes and hunting rare prints for your own binder. These are the eleven card lines you will handle. Each has its own look and its own inspection tell. Tap a card to flip it, try the variant chips to see the rare versions, and press Favorite on the ones you like.

Moth & Lantern

Lady Vesper, 014/120
Look
Victorian night print. Plum, brass and lamplight.
Tell
Hidden UV ink. Fakes stay blank under the lamp.

Neon Saints

Thunder Mama Delgado, 033/150
Look
80s arcade cabinet art. Synthwave sun, laser grid.
Tell
The holo swirl direction marks the print run. Series 2 fakes swirl the wrong way.

Grim Grocers

Count Cabbula, 007/100
Look
90s Saturday-morning cartoon. Thick outlines, cheap bright inks.
Tell
Sloppy printing. Misprints are the chase cards of this line.

Tidebound

The Drowned Bell, 052/140
Look
Old sea charts and woodcuts. Deep teal, verdigris bronze.
Tell
Waterproof stock. A real card beads water. A fake soaks it up and is ruined.

Hollow Pantheon

Ossarion, 001/060
Look
Byzantine icon. Real gold leaf on black stock.
Tell
Leaf tarnishes at a known rate, so you can date a card by eye. Fakes use gold paint.

Cryptid Field Notes

Exhibit 14: Pinewood Strider, 014/090
Look
Government case file. Grainy photo, typewriter text, paperclip.
Tell
Serial numbers must match the registry terminal. Duplicate serials mean forgeries.

Starpost

Comet Courier, 021/200
Look
Engraved postage stamp from the far future. Cream paper, two-ink print.
Tell
Count the perforations. Torn perfs drop the grade. Inverted centers are legendary errors.

Ironclad Derby

The Brass Mule #9, 009/110
Look
Sepia race-day poster. Brass, rivets and steam.
Tell
Stats changed between print runs. Check the back against the reference sheet.
The big three

Blockbuster lines

In the game world, these three lines move the whole market and fill most of your grading queue. Each follows a classic genre: a creature-collecting line, a spellcaster line and a duel-monster line. The names, creatures and frames are all original.

Bloomlings

Puddlefox, 024/102
Genre
Creature collecting. Cute elemental critters that evolve through stages.
Look
Soft pastel watercolor, silver-blue frame, chunky friendly type.
Tell
First prints have no drop shadow behind the art box. "Shadowless" copies are worth up to 10x more.

Grimoire Accord

Archivist of Ash, 147/280
Genre
Spellcaster fantasy. Mana-style costs, creature types, rules text.
Look
Painterly oil fantasy. Frame color shows the card's school of magic.
Tell
The very first "Alpha" print has rounder corners. Fakers trim corners to copy it, so you measure the radius.

Rift Duelists

Obsidian Wyrm Sovereign, RFT-EN007
Genre
Duel monsters. Level pips, ATK and DEF, dramatic anime-style beasts.
Look
Bold cel shading, neon rim light, deep crimson frame.
Tell
A silver hex hologram sits bottom right. Fakes use flat silver ink that doesn't shift under the tilt stand.
